Project 01Live on cloud server

Multi-tenant Restaurant POS

A complete point-of-sale system for restaurants: menu and tables, tap-based order taking for waiters on phones, discounts, GST, cash/card/online payments, printed receipts and daily sales reports.

  • Problem: the first version used one shared database, so every restaurant saw the same menu.
  • Solution: redesigned as multi-tenant, with a central users database and a separate SQLite database per restaurant (WAL mode, busy-timeout handling).
  • Deployment: DigitalOcean Ubuntu 24.04 server, Gunicorn behind an Nginx reverse proxy, systemd auto-restart, firewall hardening, nightly backups with 7-day retention.
  • Testing: 19-test suite for data isolation and access control, plus a 45-check end-to-end suite.

Project 02SaaS

Cloud Billing SaaS

A hosted invoicing platform with one login link for every customer. Each company signs up in about two minutes, adds its own staff, and bills clients from any device with no installation.

  • Tenant isolation: one company can never see another company's clients, invoices or payments. Cross-company access is tested automatically.
  • Roles: admins create staff logins with their own IDs and passwords and role-based permissions.
  • Accuracy: per-company invoice numbering (e.g. AB-2026-0001), automatic GST/VAT, duplicate and overpayment blocking.
  • Testing: 53 end-to-end checks, all passing.

Project 03Delivered to client

Invoice & Billing Management

Billing software built for a service company that bills its clients every month. Clients are saved once, invoices calculate themselves (units × rate, monthly charges, GST) and print on A4 with the amount in words.

  • Accounting-safe rules: unique yearly invoice numbers, duplicate invoices for the same month blocked, old invoices keep their original rates and bank details, paid invoices locked.
  • Operations: one-click next-month invoices, partial payments, outstanding and GST reports, three user roles, audit log, automatic daily backups.
  • Quality: 41-check automated verification plus 116 written test cases for user acceptance testing.

Project 04Education

School Fee Management

A fee system for schools and academies: students and classes with fee structures, one-click monthly challan generation for every student, cash/bank/online payments and printable A4 challans.

  • One click, no duplicates: generating challans skips students who already have one for that month.
  • Defaulter reports: monthly collection, class-wise totals and a list of students with dues.
  • Safety: fee changes never alter past challans, overpayments are blocked, paid challans cannot be cancelled. 39 automated checks.
